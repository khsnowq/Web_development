    About Engine 3D

    Engine 3D is an educational platform for interactive 3D visualization of industrial and scientific equipment. Students explore annotated models, understand complex machinery, and can request any model they need.

    Our team of 3D artists creates new models on demand — usually within a few days. Every model comes with annotations, theory sections, and optional tests.
